About

I started with a controller and ended up curious about what's behind the screen.

I'm Younoos, a first-year B.Tech IT student. Games have taken most of my free time for as long as I can remember, and at some point I stopped only playing them and started wondering how they work. How a character jumps. Why a level loads. Who decided the enemy would do that.

College gave that curiosity somewhere to go. I'm learning to code properly now, one small program at a time. I'm slow and stubborn about it, which helps in coding and in ranked matches.

I don't have a big portfolio yet. What I have is a lot of curiosity and a habit of sticking with a hard level until I clear it. This site is the first thing I built and put my name on.
